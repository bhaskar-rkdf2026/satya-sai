Message from the Principal

At the School of Pharmacy, our focus is on delivering quality pharmaceutical education that integrates strong theoretical foundations with practical training.

We aim to develop competent, ethical, and socially responsible pharmacists who can meet the evolving needs of healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry.

With experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, and a student-centric learning environment, the School of Pharmacy is committed to academic excellence and professional growth of every learner.
