University Support Policy for Faculty Skill Enhancement

Sri Satya Sai University of Technology & Medical Sciences (SSSUTMS) actively promotes continuous professional learning. All Assistant Professors undertaking approved NPTEL Faculty Development Programs (FDPs) receive full examination fee reimbursement upon successful course certification. Additionally, financial support is extended for attending reputed National and International FDPs and Conferences.
